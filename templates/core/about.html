About Us

Nova Vita, Nova Via

New life, new path. Nayi Raah is a journey of awareness, learning and meaningful change — turning ideas into skills, and support into action.

Why Nayi Raah

Across India, countless girls face barriers to education, wellness information and simple encouragement — not because the opportunities don't exist, but because no one has opened the door yet. Nayi Raah exists to open that door: a safe space to seek guidance, discover opportunities and know that she is never alone.

What We Do

We run conversations and sessions on topics that are often left unspoken — like menstrual hygiene — because we believe important conversations should feel open, normal and empowering. Every session is a small step, but together they build a more aware and supportive community.

Our Approach

We keep things simple and honest: real conversations over polished pamphlets, consistency over one-off events, and always meeting girls where they are. See the sessions we've run so far on our Work page.
